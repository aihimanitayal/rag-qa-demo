Getting Started

To create an account, visit the sign-up page and provide a valid email address.
A verification link is sent immediately and expires after 24 hours. If you
don't receive it, check your spam folder before requesting a new one.

Billing

Subscriptions are billed monthly in advance. You can upgrade your plan at any
time and the difference is prorated automatically. Downgrades take effect at
the start of the next billing cycle, not immediately.

Refunds are issued only within the first 14 days of a new subscription, and
only for annual plans. Monthly plans are non-refundable but can be cancelled
at any time with no further charges.

API Access

API keys are available on the Pro plan and above. Each key is rate-limited to
1000 requests per minute. Exceeding this limit returns a 429 status code, and
the limit resets every 60 seconds.

Data Export

You can export all your data as a CSV or JSON file from the account settings
page at any time. Large exports (over 100,000 records) are processed
asynchronously and emailed to you as a download link within a few hours.

Support

Support is available via email for all plans, with a typical response time of
24 to 48 hours. Priority live chat support is available exclusively on the
Enterprise plan.
