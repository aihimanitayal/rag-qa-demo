Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week, subject to manager approval.
Full remote arrangements require VP-level sign-off and are reviewed every 6 months.

All remote employees must be reachable during core hours, 10 AM to 4 PM in their
local time zone, and must attend their team's weekly sync meeting with video on.

Equipment Policy

New employees receive a laptop, monitor, and a one-time $300 home office stipend
within their first 30 days. Equipment requests after that go through the IT
helpdesk and require manager approval for anything over $150.

Leave Policy

Employees accrue 1.5 days of paid leave per month, capped at 24 days per year.
Unused leave up to 10 days can be carried over into the next calendar year.
Sick leave is separate and uncapped, but requires a doctor's note after 3
consecutive days.

Expense Reimbursement

Expenses must be submitted within 30 days of being incurred, with a receipt
attached. Reimbursements are processed within 10 business days of approval.
Travel expenses over $500 require pre-approval from a director or above.
